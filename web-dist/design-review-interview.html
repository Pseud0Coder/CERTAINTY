B

Ledger Interview

Leans hardest into the dark-academia identity: the transcript reads like a bound manuscript, annotations sit in a marginal gutter next to the exact line they judge, and voice shrinks to a single quiet toolbar at the foot of the page, a waveform, not a gadget. The debrief, not the live view, is where STAR appears.

C

Radial Focus

Voice-first, almost no chrome. The progress ring is the brand mark itself, enlarged: it fills as questions are answered, the same ring-and-check idea as the logo closing the loop. One question at a time, full width, nothing to read but that. Everything else, full transcript, STAR, waits in a drawer or the debrief.

D

Dual Card

The familiar shape: a chat card, two speakers, bubbles left and right. STAR rides as four thin bars in the header instead of a separate panel, and voice is one pill in the corner: a mic dot plus a small equalizer when live. Warmest and most approachable of the four; the smallest departure from a normal chat app.
