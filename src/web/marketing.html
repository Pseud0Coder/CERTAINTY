The recruiting platform

One spine.
Four disciplines.

01

Verify, don't assume

Every résumé line is checked against the transcript, the LinkedIn snapshot and the job description. A finding is either evidenced or it isn't. There is no in-between.

02

One pipeline, human-run

Agents suggest a stage. A recruiter advances it. The board shows exactly where every candidate stands, nothing more.

03

Measured, not guessed

STAR proportions, ownership ratio, trailing-off, scored from the transcript itself, the same way every time.

04

Nothing off the record

Every login, flag and stage change writes to an append-only audit log, scoped to its tenant, visible to admins.

Verified interviews

Scored the same
way, every time.

A live question. A console that says whether it is your turn or the model's, never that audio is happening when it isn't. STAR proportions the moment a session ends, scored by a fixed evaluator, not a guess.

  • Consent before capture. A verified session cannot record without it. Practice never saves.
  • Deterministic scoring. Situation, Task, Action, Result: the same evaluator, every session, no model in the loop.
  • Ownership measured. First person against team voice, trailing answers flagged, not eyeballed.

Built in, not bolted on

The rules live
in the code.

Quarantine on ingest

Every document is extracted, sanitized and structured before an agent ever reads it.

Projection-gated views

The candidate surface physically cannot return an internal field. It isn't hidden. It isn't there.

No invented numbers

Model output stating a figure absent from the evidence is rejected outright, not flagged for review.

Consent before capture

A verified interview cannot record without a signed consent gate. Practice sessions never save.

Suggest, never decide

Agents propose a stage, a gap, a flag. A human approves it before it becomes a task.

Append-only audit

Every mutation writes a row nobody can edit or delete. Admins can see all of it.

Tenant isolation

The tenant id comes from the session and is parameterized into every query. Always.

Deterministic fallback

A slow, wrong or absent model degrades wording. It never breaks a gate or a measurement.
