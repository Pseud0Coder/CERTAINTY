## Notes before sending
- **Send from your own address with real sender details and an unsubscribe/opt-out line.** Don't use purchased lists. Only email business addresses that are publicly listed or that you found legitimately, and honour opt-outs. Check current rules (IT Act, DPDP) with counsel if you scale up.
- Only claim what you can show. "Pilots" is fine; don't state acceptance-rate results until you have them.
- Personalise the first line per firm (use the `focus` column). Send 20 to 30 a day from a warmed-up domain, not 100 at once.
- Follow-up: one reminder after 4 days, a last note after 10, then stop.
