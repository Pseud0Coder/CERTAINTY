            visible to admins.

[p] Built in, not bolted on
[h2] The rules live
[h2] in the code.
[h3] Quarantine on ingest
[p] Every document is extracted, sanitized and structured before an agent ever reads it.
[h3] Projection-gated views
[p] The candidate surface physically cannot return an internal field. It isn't hidden. It isn't there.
[h3] No invented numbers
[p] Model output stating a figure absent from the evidence is rejected outright, not flagged for review.
[h3] Consent before capture
[p] A verified interview cannot record without a signed consent gate. Practice sessions never save.
[h3] Suggest, never decide
[p] Agents propose a stage, a gap, a flag. A human approves it before it becomes a task.
[h3] Append-only audit
[p] Every mutation writes a row nobody can edit or delete. Admins can see all of it.
[h3] Tenant isolation
[p] The tenant id comes from the session and is parameterized into every query. Always.
[h3] Deterministic fallback
[p] A slow, wrong or absent model degrades wording. It never breaks a gate or a measurement.
